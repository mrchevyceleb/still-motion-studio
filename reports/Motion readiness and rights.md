## Fresh CC0 weights replace the unresolved permission chain

CondMDI explicitly licenses its **code** under MIT and reserves separate dependency/dataset terms. Its released models train on HumanML3D, which incorporates AMASS-derived motion. AMASS's public terms restrict commercial-use neural-network training and dataset redistribution. Those terms do not alone resolve every downstream question about pretrained weights or generated outputs, but they prevent us from asserting commercial clearance. We prepared requests covering the exact checkpoint, commercial inference, distribution, modification, customer outputs, and upstream contributor permissions. **The requests remain unsent.** ([CondMDI scope](https://github.com/setarehc/diffusion-motion-inbetweening#license), [AMASS terms](https://amass.is.tue.mpg.de/license.html), [permission drafts](<C:/Users/MattJ/Documents/ChatGPT/Animation Rigger/docs/model-permission-requests.md>))

The concrete alternative uses Quaternius' creator-published CC0 humanoid and animal animation, random network initialization, and training-only statistics. Source files, splits, code, and checkpoints have recorded hashes. The release helper passes these two exact model lineages and rejects unresolved or research-only alternatives; it does not block local inference. This documents a permission basis for those model assets, not a complete product legal or dependency certification. DART reserves AMASS/SMPL obligations despite its Apache model grant; AnyTop and UniMate publish expressly non-commercial checkpoint terms. ([Quaternius animal pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html), [humanoid pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html), [CC0 scope](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en), [release manifest](<C:/Users/MattJ/Documents/ChatGPT/Animation Rigger/backend/model-rights.json>), [DART](https://github.com/zkf1997/DART#license), [AnyTop weights](https://huggingface.co/inbar2344/AnyTop/raw/main/LICENSE), [UniMate weights](https://huggingface.co/Linzhan/UniMate/raw/main/LICENSE))

## The animal model is useful within a narrow tested family

**Still Quadruped** trained an 855,862-parameter conditional denoiser on 126 authored clips from ten animals. All 13 horse clips were held out, and the duplicate white-horse asset was excluded. Across 39 windows with six observed poses, normalized missing-frame RMS error fell from **0.06496 to 0.02255, a 65.3% reduction** against interpolation. Changing the seed changes missing motion; changing a front-leg key affects other legs, demonstrating learned conditional behavior. This measures generalization within one stylized animation family, not realistic biomechanics or arbitrary animal anatomy. ([Corpus and model](<C:/Users/MattJ/Documents/ChatGPT/Animation Rigger/research_notes/Motion readiness and rights/learned-quadrupeds.md>), [executed measurements](<C:/Users/MattJ/Documents/ChatGPT/Animation Rigger/.local/quadruped/validation-proof.json>))

The app-shaped quadruped test preserved six keys and source bone lengths, and a 12-second/four-window test returned 361 frames with exact anchors. The held-out 50-bone horse retained its five roots and existing animated local offsets. The small humanoid model also passed structural round trips, but its 65-clip training corpus contained only about 107 seconds of motion. Across 16 held-out clips/48 windows, its **0.06728 error exceeded interpolation's 0.04298**. It remains clearly marked experimental and should not become the default on quality grounds. Neither model is a physics solver. ## Contact locking improves feet without certifying dynamics

The independent MuJoCo provider now refines inferred stance contacts and remeasures the full returned motion. On the supplied **Reaction** animation, mean contact sliding fell from **0.22855 m/s to 0.00003595 m/s**, and maximum sole penetration fell from **122.38 mm to 4.00 mm**. However, root-force residual remained roughly **44,219 N**, down from 85,674 N. That is a substantial contact repair with an unmistakable review requirement, not physically feasible character motion. ([Reaction measurements](<C:/Users/MattJ/Documents/ChatGPT/Animation Rigger/.local/user-fixtures/reaction-physics.json>))

The static **Bear** test was much better conditioned: root-force residual fell from **686.7 N to 0.823 N**, with 0.612 mm maximum soft-contact compression and nearly zero sliding; it passed the proxy's configured thresholds. On the earlier 18.2-second dance fixture, contact refinement reduced sliding from 0.09173 m/s to about 0.000005 m/s, but 955 N root-force residual still required review. Masses, anatomy, contact proxies, and joint limits remain approximations. Stronger foot planting does not remove the need to calibrate the physical body or review impossible timing. ([Bear measurements](<C:/Users/MattJ/Documents/ChatGPT/Animation Rigger/.local/user-fixtures/bear-physics.json>), [dance refinement](<C:/Users/MattJ/Documents/ChatGPT/Animation Rigger/.local/contact-refinement/final-result.json>))

## Conclusion

Commercial independence now has an implemented route: expand the explicitly permitted corpus and train new weights while preserving its provenance. The useful next investment is broader motion coverage and proportion/contact evaluation, especially for humanoids, rather than treating a small rights-cleared checkpoint as a finished animator. Keep model permission checks and measured quality checks separate so that progress on one never conceals a failure in the other.
