# Community conduct

Treat people with respect. Welcome different skill levels and backgrounds, give specific constructive feedback, and focus disagreement on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not welcome. Do not use issues or pull requests for spam or personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect contributors. Use GitHub's **Report content** tools for abuse that needs private escalation. Do not repost private information while reporting it.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
