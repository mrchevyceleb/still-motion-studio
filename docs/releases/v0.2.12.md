Install **Still-Motion-Studio-Setup-0.2.12.exe** on Windows x64, or choose **Updates** in an existing installation.

- **Rig hands and fingers:** import an unrigged humanoid in a neutral pose, choose **Add a rig → Detect body & finger joints**, inspect both hands, and choose **Bind hands & body**. Each hand has five digits, with three bend joints and a tip per digit. Drag landmarks or enter coordinates before binding.
- **Review uncertain placement:** open, separated fingers improve geometry detection. Estimated fingertips are marked for correction; gloves and fused fingers need manual placement. Body-only fitting remains available, and existing imported rigs are preserved.
- **Pose individual fingers:** show finger joints and focus on a selected joint. Fingertip movement stays within the finger chain. Hand poses save with the scene and export with the animated rig.
- **Edit a take:** open **Clip tools** to trim, change speed, reverse, or keep planar hip movement in place while retaining height and turns.
- **Build longer motion:** close a loop seam, repeat a take while accumulating its position and heading, or blend an embedded clip or matching saved `.still` take onto the current trajectory. Saved takes must use the same character and bind rig.
- Each clip operation has one Undo and saves with the scene. GLB and Unreal exports use the edited motion. Review contacts after blending and rerun physics after clip edits to refresh measurements.

Includes bulk keyframe corrections, the scene library, and guided physics comparisons from earlier releases. The practice humanoid keeps its existing skeleton for saved-scene compatibility. The standalone humanoid AI model remains excluded by its motion-quality gate.

The installer is unsigned and Windows x64 only. Matching `.blockmap`, `latest.yml`, `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and `release-source.json` assets support verified automatic updates. No user scenes or GitHub credentials are included.
