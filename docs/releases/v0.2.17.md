Install **Still-Motion-Studio-Setup-0.2.17.exe** on Windows x64, or choose **Updates** in an existing installation.

- **Saved characters, then scenes:** open **Characters** to find your rigged models. Each character has its own collection of animations, with clear scene counts and thumbnail previews.
- **Reuse the same model:** choose **New scene** inside a character, or **New scene with this character** in the editor. The scene starts with neutral poses and the saved model, skin and rig. No reimporting or rerigging.
- **Manage your scenes:** open, rename, duplicate and delete scenes inside their character. Duplicates preserve the complete animation. You can delete the current or last scene; the character stays saved for later.
- **Find older work:** existing saved scenes are grouped automatically by their model and rig. Search characters or scene names, and use the character breadcrumb to return to its scenes.
- **Keep characters locked:** renaming a character changes its label. Swapping its look creates a new character and scene while preserving the original character and animations. Unrigged imports remain drafts until binding succeeds.
- **Move, copy and paste keyframes:** visible timeline controls let you enter a destination time, copy a pose or selected poses, and paste at the playhead. Ctrl C/V also work. Pasting or dragging onto an occupied frame replaces that pose, with one Undo.
- **Duplicated poses reach their intended frame:** dragging a duplicate on a dense timeline no longer sends it to a distant empty frame. Its pose plays at the new time.
- **Cut the ending easily:** choose **End clip here**, **Remove empty tail**, or set **Clip end** in seconds. Earlier keyframes retain their times. Duration changes trim instead of rescaling the animation; deleting ending keyframes also preserves retained playback.

Includes the animation import, appearance swap, existing-skeleton Unreal export and app-only update features from previous releases. Local scenes, recovery backups and custom runtime models remain in user data. Export a `.still` scene for a portable backup with its character included.

The full Setup installer includes the validated offline runtime; the Update installer reuses a matching installed runtime. Both include matching blockmaps, a verified update feed, hashes and source identity. Windows x64; installers are unsigned.
