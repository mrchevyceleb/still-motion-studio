Install **Still-Motion-Studio-Setup-0.2.19.exe**, or choose **Updates** in Still.

- **Fit a part:** under **Your character**, choose **Fit a part**, pick a piece of the look (listed by material, such as a pot or a hat) and type how far to move it up, forward or left in cm, how far to turn it, or its size. The viewport previews it live; **Apply** keeps it. Values are measured on the rest pose, facing forward.
- **Rides its bones:** the fit is baked into the mesh through its own skin binding, so the part follows its bones in every pose, the saved scene, the animated GLB and the Unreal export. Bones, rest pose and keys never change. A fitted look saves as its own character, like Swap look; scenes of the original keep its look. **Put this part back** undoes every fit on that part.
- **Speed up range only:** in the clip tools, set **Start** and **End** around part of a clip, type a speed (above 1 is faster) and choose **Speed up range only**. Only that stretch changes speed, easing in and out at its edges, and everything after it moves earlier or later to match. Poses are untouched. Good for a jump that floats at the top.

Includes keyboard shortcuts from 0.2.18 and saved characters and scenes, keyframe copy/paste, exact-frame moving and clip trimming from 0.2.17. Full and app-only Windows x64 installers include verified update metadata.
