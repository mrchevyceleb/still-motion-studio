Install **Still-Motion-Studio-Setup-0.2.19.exe**, or choose **Updates** in Still.

- **Move model** is now a labelled tool directly on the board. Click it or press **G**, then drag any part of the character to move the whole model across the floor.
- **Shift-drag** raises or lowers the whole model. Joint markers are hidden while moving the model. Press **W**, **E** or **Esc** to return to joint editing.
- The move changes the current pose only, preserves bone rotations and keyframe timing, and creates one Undo step. **Escape during a drag** cancels it without saving.
- Use **Position & property tools → Current pose position** for exact coordinates, or **Same correction** to apply a repositioning to selected keyframes.

Includes saved characters and scenes, keyframe copy/paste, clip trimming and the full keyboard shortcut guide. Full and app-only Windows x64 installers include verified update metadata.
