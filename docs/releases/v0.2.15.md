Install **Still-Motion-Studio-Setup-0.2.15.exe** on Windows x64, or choose **Updates** in an existing installation.

- **Put a Mixamo animation on your loaded character:** choose **Import animation** under **Your character**, select an animated FBX or GLB, and choose **Apply to character**. Animation-only files and files with skin both work.
- **Build attack → recovery sequences:** choose **Append after current motion** and set the transition duration. Zero makes a direct cut. Clip tools also has an **Append animation FBX or GLB** entry.
- **Keep your character:** importing motion preserves the current mesh, rig, materials and skin. Undo restores the previous take, and the result saves and exports with the scene.
- **Clearer import choices:** **Import a character** opens a new scene; **Import animation** changes motion in the current scene. The dialog explains which Mixamo download to use.

Animation transfer requires a matching rig. Names can differ by Mixamo namespace, and duplicate names are checked against their joint hierarchy. Different skeletons, proportions, bind poses or coordinate transforms require retargeting first; unsupported motion channels are rejected.

Includes the app-only update support and existing-skeleton Unreal export from 0.2.14. The installers are unsigned and Windows x64 only. `Still-Motion-Studio-Setup-0.2.15.exe` is the full installer; `Still-Motion-Studio-Update-0.2.15.exe` is the app-only update. Matching blockmaps, `latest.yml`, hashes, and source identity support verified automatic updates. No user scenes or GitHub credentials are included.
