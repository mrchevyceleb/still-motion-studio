Install **Still-Motion-Studio-Setup-0.2.20.exe**, or choose **Updates** in Still.

- **Fit a part** under Your character adjusts the position, rotation and size of one mesh without changing its rig or keyframes. Apply keeps it in the saved character and exports; closing cancels a preview.
- **Speed up range only** in Clip tools changes the selected time range, easing into and out of the speed change. Motion outside the range keeps its pace.
- Zoom now follows the mouse pointer by default. **Viewport settings → Zoom to mouse position** turns this off and remembers the choice.
- Extending a clip keeps the timeline's visual scale and every keyframe timestamp. Use **Fit clip**, **+** or **−** to change the timeline view; use **End** to reach the added space.
- Includes **Move model (G)**, character/scene libraries, keyframe controls, trimming and keyboard shortcuts from the previous releases.
