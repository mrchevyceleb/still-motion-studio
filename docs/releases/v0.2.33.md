# Still Motion Studio 0.2.33

- Copy your own movement with Motion Capture. Drop in a phone video or record with your camera, and Still tracks your body entirely on this device and poses a human character to match. Nothing is uploaded.
- Trim the clip, pick a sample rate and smoothing, then preview, Apply or Append like any library clip. Every pose stays editable, and one Undo restores your previous take. Mirror suits flipped selfie videos, and side-to-side floor travel can follow the video.
- Best with one whole body in frame, head to feet, and a steady camera. Fast spins, floor work and walking toward the camera are copied loosely. Hand and finger motion is not tracked, and quadruped or creature characters cannot use it yet.
- The on-device tracker (Google MediaPipe Pose Landmarker, Apache-2.0) is included in the installer. Motion Library and Motion Capture previews now share one owner, so Apply always matches the preview you are watching.
